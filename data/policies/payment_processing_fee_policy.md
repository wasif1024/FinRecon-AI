# Payment Processing Fee Policy

Owner: Financial Operations. Applies to all card payments processed through PayGate on behalf of merchants. Synthetic policy for the FinRecon AI demonstration.

## 1. Expected fee structure

The contracted PayGate rate is 2.9% of the gross amount plus a fixed 0.30 per successful payment, in the currency of the payment. The fee recorded by the gateway at authorisation is the expected fee. The settlement must deduct exactly that fee from the gross amount.

Cross-border and premium-card surcharges are permitted only when they appear as separate line items in the PayGate fee breakdown for that payment. A surcharge that is not itemised is treated as an unexplained fee difference.

## 2. Fee discrepancy thresholds

Fee differences are assessed per payment, in the payment currency. Never combine differences across currencies.

- **Up to 0.50**: record as an explained variance. No approval is required, but the case must note the amount and the settlement reference.
- **More than 0.50 and up to 25.00**: request the itemised PayGate fee breakdown before resolution. Resolution requires approval by a Financial Operations Manager.
- **More than 25.00**: escalate to the PayGate account manager and the Finance Controller within 2 business days.

### 2.1 Repeated discrepancies

Three or more fee discrepancies for the same merchant within one calendar month must be escalated as a pattern, regardless of individual amounts. The escalation should list every affected settlement.

## 3. Disputing a fee

A fee dispute is raised with PayGate through the merchant support portal and must include the payment ID, the settlement ID, the expected fee, the charged fee and the itemised breakdown if one was provided. Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the settlement date; PayGate does not accept older disputes.

## 4. Documentation

Every closed fee case must retain: the gateway payment record, the settlement record, the fee breakdown (if requested), and the approver's decision. Records are retained for seven years.
