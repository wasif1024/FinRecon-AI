# Currency mismatch

The settlement and the bank credit are in different currencies.

## Check
1. Both currencies and amounts exactly as recorded. Never convert, add or net amounts across currencies, and do not judge whether the amounts are "equivalent".
2. Whether other credits from the same bank show the same mismatch.
3. `search_policies` for currency mismatches: what must be requested and when the case may close. Cite it.

## Usual outcomes
Request a correction from the bank and keep the case open until the corrected credit arrives; escalate if the policy requires it.
